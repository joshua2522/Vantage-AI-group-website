FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the 30-minute consultation really free?

Yes — completely. It's a no-obligation call to understand your business and see if we're a good fit. There's no charge and no pressure to commit.

Why don't you list prices on the site?

Because every build is different. A fixed price list would either overcharge simple work or under-deliver on complex needs. We scope your exact requirements on the free call and give you an honest, fixed quote.

Do I own the software you build?

Yes. The software we build for you is yours, including the code. No lock-in and no hostage situations — if you ever want to take it elsewhere, you can.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

Not at all. We handle the technical side and explain everything in plain language. Your job is to know your business — ours is to build the software.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused system can launch in a few weeks; a full platform takes longer. You always get a clear timeline and fixed quote before any work begins.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Security and privacy are built in from day one. Your data stays yours, and we follow best practices for protection and compliance.
