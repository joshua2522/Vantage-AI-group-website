Yes — completely. It's a no-obligation call to understand your needs and see if we're a good fit. There's no charge and no pressure to commit.

Because every project is different. A fixed price list would either overcharge simple work or under-deliver on complex needs. We scope your exact requirements on the free call and give you an honest, transparent quote.

Do I need technical knowledge to work with you?

Not at all. We handle the technical side and explain everything in plain language. Your job is to know your business — ours is to bring the AI.

How long does a typical project take?

Smaller automations can launch in a couple of weeks; larger transformations take longer. We always give you a clear timeline before any work begins.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Security and privacy are built into everything we do. Your data stays yours, and we follow best practices for protection and compliance.
